Chapter 01
The beginning

The Birth of ODP — The First Regime

ODP's history began with Omran Omer, Toni Sadriu, Behrad Riazati and the early members of their Fortnite community. What started as friends playing together gradually developed into a political system.

Toni Sadriu became the first supreme leader. After roughly a year, corruption and political tensions grew. Omran launched a rebellion, overthrew Toni's government and imprisoned him. Later, Omran released Toni, allowing him to return to politics. Behrad, once Toni's loyal assistant, remained an important early figure.

April 20
Turning point

The Night of Chaos

Behrad Riazati, previously one of Toni's closest assistants, changed political sides and betrayed him. The event reshaped relationships among the original leadership. After his release, Toni joined Omran's newly established Omran Democratic Party.

Chapter 03
Golden age

The Golden Age of Omran

Under Omran's leadership, ODP expanded its government and brought new members into leadership and parliament. Toni returned as an influential figure. Simon Nilsen Sebastian became Minister of Attack, while Toni Sadriu became Minister of Defence.

The organisation appeared united and powerful, and its institutions became increasingly established.

Chapter 04
Expansion

The Expansion of the ODP Empire

Omran's ambitions reached beyond the original nation. ODP spread its influence into new territories and, at its height, controlled approximately 25 countries. Its economy and political reach grew to unprecedented levels within the storyline.

Chapter 05
Authoritarian turn

The Beginning of the Dictatorship

Over time, Omran became increasingly paranoid. Freedom of speech was restricted, criticism was no longer tolerated, and the democratic principles that had shaped ODP began to disappear.

“You have freedom of speech, but I can't guarantee freedom after speech.”

Stories about the treatment of political opponents — including rumours about prisoners and Omran's lions — contributed to the regime's frightening reputation. These darker stories remain unverified within the narrative.

Chapter 06
Growing opposition

The Betrayal of Simon and the Rise of the Rebels

Sarunas became a prominent opponent and formed a rebel organisation. Simon Nilsen Sebastian, formerly Minister of Attack, joined the rebellion. Khurram Hayat also aligned with the opposition after losing his assistant position. Marius Johansen became associated with the rebels, though the details were unclear.

Toni remained an important insider, increasingly concerned about ODP's direction, while Behrad had returned to a position of loyalty. The government began to fracture from within.

Chapter 07
Failed restoration

The Failed Democratic Restoration

Toni and Behrad attempted to restore democratic principles through elections. Behrad won the presidential election, but Omran refused to recognise the result and prevented him from taking office. The election exposed how little independence ODP's democratic institutions retained.

Chapter 08
Civil conflict

The Final Collapse

Toni left ODP and established a separate movement. Khurram Hayat, Marius Johansen and Sarunas Smitides joined him. Conflict escalated, and ODP — once a powerful organisation with around 25 territories — struggled to survive. Its political unity had disappeared.

Chapter 09
Unfinished ending

Operation Omran's Capture

As the conflict reached its final stages, an operation was initiated to locate and capture Omran Omer. The operation was never completed. Within the ODP storyline, Omran's whereabouts remain unknown — one of its greatest unanswered questions.

Chapter 10
Historical legacy

The Legacy of ODP

ODP began as a group of friends and became an elaborate political world of governments, elections, ministers, territorial expansion and international conflicts. Its ambition and concentration of power eventually undermined the unity and democratic ideals on which it was founded.

It was once an empire of 25 territories. Now, it remains a memory of what it once was.
